IPTV Information & Resources

What Is IPTV?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It refers to television and video content delivered through an internet connection instead of traditional terrestrial, satellite or cable delivery methods.

What Internet Speed Do You Need?

Streaming requirements depend on video resolution, device performance and network conditions. A stable broadband connection is important for smooth playback, particularly when watching high-resolution video.

What Is an EPG?

EPG stands for Electronic Program Guide. It provides program information such as channel schedules, program titles and broadcast times when supported.

Which Devices Can I Use?

Compatible devices may include Smart TVs, Android TV, Fire TV, Apple devices, tablets, Windows computers and other supported streaming hardware.
