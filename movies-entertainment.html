Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about the movie collection, compatible devices and setup.

How often is the movies and series collection updated?+

The entertainment collection is updated regularly. Browse the titles currently listed on this page.

Which devices can I use to watch?+

Supported options include Smart TVs, Android TV, Fire TV, Android and Apple devices, Windows, macOS and web browsers. Compatibility can depend on your app and setup.

Are movies and TV series available in HD or Full HD?+

HD and Full HD are available for supported titles. Actual playback quality can vary by title, source, device and connection.

Is 4K available for every title?+

No. 4K depends on the title and requires a compatible device and setup. Not every title is available in every quality.

Can I use multiple devices at once?+

Device use and simultaneous connections depend on your selected plan. Check the plan details or contact support for guidance.

Where can I get installation or setup help?+

Read our IPTV installation guides or contact our support team.
